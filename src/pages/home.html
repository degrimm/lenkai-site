Classes

Where your child fits

Lenkai follows Kenya’s Competency‑Based Curriculum, so your child can stay with us from their first day of school to Senior School.

Pre‑Primary

PP1 and PP2. Early reading, numbers, play and creative activities for the youngest children.

Primary

Grade 1 to Grade 6. English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, CRE and Creative Arts. KPSEA at Grade 6.

Junior School

Grade 7 to Grade 9. On the same campus, ending with KJSEA at Grade 9.

Senior School

Grade 10 from 2026, Grade 11 from 2027. We are also carrying our Form 3 and Form 4 students through to KCSE.

How to join Lenkai →

Day or boarding

Come each day, or live at school.

Day pupils come from Kimana and the villages around it. A school vehicle with its own driver serves the Kimana area; ask us which routes it covers.

Boarders live at school during term. This year 65 girls and 52 boys board, looked after by a Boarding Head, with meals cooked by the school chef and caterer.
