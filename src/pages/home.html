Lenkai Christian School teaches Kenya’s Competency‑Based curriculum from PP1 to Senior School, to families from across Kajiado and over the border in Tanzania. It is also a refuge: girls escaping FGM and forced marriage live and learn here.

On campus

What your child gets here

Two computer labs

An ICT laboratory worth roughly KSh 6 million, given in 2019 by an American family. Two labs, split by age group, plus two offices.

Boarding for girls and boys

117 boarders this year, 65 girls and 52 boys, under a dedicated Rescue & Boarding Head, with a school chef and caterer.

Music, drama and games

Staff lead music, drama and poetry, and a Games Head runs sport. Lenkai competes, and Lenkai sings. A school vehicle serves the Kimana area; ask us about routes.

500+

girls rescued from FGM and childhood marriage in Kajiado County by the Hope Beyond Foundation, working alongside the school.

More than a school

Some of our girls came here running.

Lenkai grew from five pupils into a full school because girls kept arriving, rescued from female genital mutilation and from being married off as children. We took them in, and we kept teaching.

Today a rescued girl can stay at Lenkai from the day she arrives until she finishes Senior School.
