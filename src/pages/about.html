About the school

A school built around one verse.

Lenkai Christian School is a co‑educational day and boarding school in Kimana, Kajiado County, teaching from Pre‑Primary through Senior School and building intellectual knowledge, skills, attitudes and Christian values in every child.

Who we are

Lenkai is a Christian day and boarding school serving families in and around Kimana, Kajiado County. It is co‑educational throughout, in class and in the boarding wing, and it runs from Pre‑Primary through Junior and Senior School under Kenya’s Competency‑Based Education curriculum. It exists to develop the whole child: what they know, what they can do, how they carry themselves, and what they believe.

How we started

The school opened with five pupils. It has grown into a full school of around 300 day pupils and boarders, from Pre‑Primary to Senior School. From the start it has also taken in girls who had nowhere safe to go, and given them a home and a place in class. Read about the rescue centre.

Who leads it

Lenkai was founded and is led by Rev. John Parit and Dorcus Parit. Rev. Parit also founded the Hope Beyond Foundation, which works alongside the school.

What we teach
