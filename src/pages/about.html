About us

Lenkai school is transforming lives every day.

A Christian school in the Kimana area of Kajiado County, teaching girls and boys from Pre‑Primary to Senior School.

Our Story

Lenkai Christian School is a co‑educational day and boarding school offering Kenya’s Competency‑Based Curriculum, with a strong emphasis on developing intellectual knowledge, skills, attitudes and Christian values of each individual child. The school is located in the Kimana area.

The school opened with five pupils. Today it teaches around 300 day pupils and boarders, from Pre‑Primary to Senior School.

Who leads it

Lenkai was founded and is led by Rev. John Parit and Dorcus Parit. Rev. Parit also founded the Hope Beyond Foundation, which works alongside the school. About the rescue centre.
