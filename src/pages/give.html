Four ways to help

Choose what you want your gift to do

Sponsor a girl

Covers one rescued girl’s boarding, meals, uniform and school materials for a full year. The most direct way to keep a girl out of a forced marriage.

Carry her through Senior School

Lenkai now teaches through Senior School, so a rescued girl can finish where she started, if her final years are funded. This is where sponsorship most often runs out.

Give once

Any amount, whenever you can. It goes into the general fund: food, books, salaries, repairs, whatever the school is short of that term.

Give in kind

Books, computers, sports equipment, building work. Talk to us before you ship anything.

Accountability

We will tell you where it went.

Before you give, ask us three things: who runs the school, what we did with the last gift, and whether you can see the numbers. We would rather answer all three than ask you to take us on trust.

Lenkai was founded and is led by Rev. John Parit and Dorcus Parit, and works alongside the Hope Beyond Foundation, which has rescued more than 500 girls in Kajiado County.
