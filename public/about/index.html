About the school

A school built around one verse.

Lenkai Christian School is a co‑educational day and boarding school in Kimana, Kajiado County, teaching from Pre‑Primary through Senior School and building intellectual knowledge, skills, attitudes and Christian values in every child.

Who we are

Lenkai is a Christian day and boarding school serving families in and around Kimana, at the foot of Kilimanjaro and close to the Tanzanian border. It is co‑educational throughout, in class and in the boarding wing, and it runs from Pre‑Primary through Junior and Senior School under Kenya’s Competency‑Based Education curriculum. It exists to develop the whole child: what they know, what they can do, how they carry themselves, and what they believe.

How we started

The school opened with five pupils. It grew because girls rescued from female genital mutilation and early marriage were brought here and needed somewhere to learn. Enrolment climbed, and Lenkai became both a school and a home.

Who leads it

Lenkai was founded and is led by Rev. John Parit and Dorcus Parit. Rev. Parit also founded the Hope Beyond Foundation, which has rescued more than 500 girls from FGM and childhood marriage in Kajiado County alone.

Curriculum

What we teach

Lenkai teaches every level of Kenya’s Competency‑Based Education pathway on one campus, and is carrying the country’s last two 8‑4‑4 cohorts through to their KCSE.
