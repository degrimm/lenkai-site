Admission to Lenkai

We admit throughout the year where there is space. The quickest way to start is to call or WhatsApp the school, or to walk in on any school day.

WhatsApp the school Call +254 725 501 002

How it works

Four steps, and you can start today

Call, WhatsApp or visit

Reach us on +254 725 501 002, by call or WhatsApp, or come to the school gate on a school day.

Tour the school with us

See the classrooms, the computer labs, the kitchen and the boarding wing. Ask the teachers anything. Bring your child; we would rather they saw it too.

Bring the paperwork

Birth certificate or notification, immunisation card for early years, and a leaving certificate or last report form if your child is transferring in.

Confirm the place and the fees

We will tell you the term’s fees, what the uniform costs, and what your child needs to bring on the first day.

Fees

Ask us for this term’s fees

Fees depend on your child’s level and on whether they board. Call or WhatsApp the school office and we will send you the current fee structure for day and boarding pupils.

Families from Tanzania

Coming from over the border?

Kimana is close to the Tanzanian border, and families from Tanzania are welcome, as day pupils or as boarders.

Calling from Tanzania: dial +254 725 501 002, or message the same number on WhatsApp.

Placement: Kenya teaches the Competency‑Based curriculum. We assess every child who joins from another system and place them in the class that fits where they actually are, so bring your child’s most recent school reports.

Documents: bring your child’s passport or birth certificate. Ask us before you travel about the paperwork a pupil from Tanzania needs to study in Kenya, and we will tell you what to prepare.

Before you travel

Message us first

Tell us your child’s age, their current class, and whether they will board. We will reply with the right class, the fees and the documents to bring.

Questions parents ask

Before you call

Do you take both boys and girls?

Yes. Lenkai is co‑educational from Pre‑Primary through Senior School, in class and in the boarding wing. The rescue programme is for girls.

Do we have to be Christian?

No. Lenkai is a Christian school and Christian values run through the day: assembly, CRE, and how we treat one another. Families of any background are welcome, and we will be honest with you about what your child will take part in.

Can my child board?

Yes. Both girls and boys board. This year 65 girls and 52 boys live at school alongside our day pupils, with a dedicated Rescue & Boarding Head, a school chef and a caterer. Ask about space when you visit.

Is there a school bus?

Yes, the school runs a vehicle with its own driver serving the Kimana area. Ask us which routes we currently cover.

When does the school year start?

Lenkai follows Kenya’s national school calendar of three terms, starting in January. We admit during the year where there is space, so call or WhatsApp us for this year’s term dates.

Can I see the school before I decide?

Please do. Visitors are welcome on campus on any school day. If you are travelling from outside Kimana, call first so someone is free to walk you round.
